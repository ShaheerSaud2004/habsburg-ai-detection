Here's what you'll need: - A high-resolution digital SLR camera with an interchangeable lens - Tripod and remote shutter release cable (to eliminate any movement during exposure) - Automated panoramic head mount for your tripod First, choose your subject carefully - something with plenty of visual interest works best. You may want to shoot from multiple angles as well, so plan out each position before starting. Set up your equipment according to these guidelines and start taking pictures! It’s important not only overlap every frame by at least 50% but also keep consistent distances between them throughout all shots taken around object/scene chosen.
